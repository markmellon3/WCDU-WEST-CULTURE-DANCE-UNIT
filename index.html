WHO WE ARE

More Than a
Dance Unit.

West Culture Dance Unit (WCDU) is a high-energy dance collective based in Kampala, specializing in hip-hop, contemporary fusion, and street styles. Our mission is to inspire, create, and perform with unmatched intensity and passion.

We don't just collect support — we actively distribute it to create real, lasting change in our communities across Kampala, the Central Region, and surrounding areas. Through movement, we build culture, community, and impact.
